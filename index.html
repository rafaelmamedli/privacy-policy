Almarket — account deletion

To delete your account in the Almarket app: Profile → Delete account → confirm.

If you do not have access to the app, write to info@almarket.az including your phone number — deleted within 30 days.

Deleted: name, phone, email, profile picture, addresses, favorites, search history, notification token.

Retained: order and payment documents — 5 years due to tax legislation (unlinked from the account); anonymous statistics — 14 months. Permanently deleted after these periods.
